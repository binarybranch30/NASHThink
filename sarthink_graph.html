Reading the graph

Colour is the platform. Filled dots are people; rings are threads (conversations, chats, posts). A line means that person wrote in that thread. Toggle platforms and node types in the sidebar, and narrow the timeline to the months you care about.

Memory search runs locally against your index and highlights the matching threads and their participants. Everything stays on this machine.

Ask Sarthink answers a question with a short brief built only from quotes of the retrieved memories, with a confidence level, a timeline and the sources. It respects the platform toggles and the timeline range. No language model writes the answer, so it never adds facts the sources don't show.
